Your airport taxi in Bahrain

Once you’re on the ground — or packing for departure — our Bahraini team of trustworthy, respectful and professional drivers meets you at the terminal and takes you to your hotel or address, or gets you to the airport on time for your flight. Need a ride back later? We arrange return trips too.

We do not publish fixed prices. Tell us your pickup point, drop-off and flight time on WhatsApp and we will send a quote for your trip. Normal family cars are the default; if you need a larger vehicle, just ask and we arrange it.
