Land at Bahrain International Airport and go straight to Dammam, Khobar or Dhahran — or fly into King Fahd International Airport (Dammam Airport) and continue to Bahrain. Yellow Taxi Bahrain also covers city-to-city trips both ways across the King Fahd Causeway.

Airport transfers and city-to-city trips

We pick you up at Bahrain International Airport arrivals and drive you straight to Dammam, Khobar, Dhahran or King Fahd International Airport — and bring you from Dammam Airport back to Bahrain. Our Bahraini team of trustworthy, respectful and professional drivers also runs door-to-door city trips between Bahrain and the Eastern Province when you are not flying that day.

We do not publish fixed prices. Send your pickup and drop-off on WhatsApp and we will quote that journey. Family cars are standard; ask if you need a larger vehicle.
